# CNI Research Contribution Guidelines

This repository showcases publicly releasable research code and supporting materials developed by students and research scholars of the Centre for Neuroinformatics (CNI), VIT.

## 1. Repository Organization

Research is organized year-wise:

- `2024/Patents/`
- `2024/Papers/`
- `2025/Patents/`
- `2025/Papers/`
- `2026/Patents/`
- `2026/Papers/`

Choose the correct publication year and category before adding your work.

## 2. Project Folder Naming

Create one folder for your own patent or paper.

Use:

`Short_Project_Title_StudentName`

Example:

`EEG_Biometric_Authentication_Kanimozhi`

Avoid names such as:

- `final`
- `final_code`
- `new`
- `project1`
- `test`
- `latest`
